# Code of conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, respectful collaboration environment. Be precise about evidence, criticize
work rather than people, respect privacy and do not pressure maintainers to disclose credentials or concealed test
data.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate evidence falsification,
credential publication, sealed-data exfiltration and attempts to bypass safety controls.

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through the repository host. Maintainers may edit or reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove participants when necessary to protect the
community and project.
